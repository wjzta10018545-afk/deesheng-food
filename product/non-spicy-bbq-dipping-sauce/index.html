Non-spicy BBQ Dipping Sauce Wholesale

Deesheng Food supplies non-spicy BBQ dipping sauce for restaurant, distribution and private-label enquiries. Its confirmed formats are 400 g × 30 and 1 kg × 10, each with a 12-month shelf life and ambient storage. Is this the same as the general BBQ Sauce?+

No. This is the non-spicy dipping variant. The BBQ Sauce offered for marinating, dipping and grilling has a separate formula and specification.

Does non-spicy mean zero chilli or zero SHU?+

The name does not establish those claims. Request the ingredient declaration and evaluate a sample against your sensory requirements.

Which packs are confirmed for this flavor?+

The confirmed non-spicy packs are 400 g × 30 and 1 kg × 10. Ask for flavor-specific confirmation of any additional series format.

Can I use it as a marinade or grilling glaze?+

Those applications require separate product guidance and testing. This dipping-sauce specification does not establish a marinade ratio or grilling performance.

What shelf life and storage apply?+

Both confirmed packs have a 12-month shelf life with ambient storage. Request the approved label for temperature limits and handling after opening.

How do I check ingredients, allergens and certification?+

Request the current ingredient list, allergen declaration and any documents required for your destination. Certification must match the manufacturing site, selected product, formula and current certificate scope. A product name or pack size does not establish dietary suitability.

Can it be supplied for private label?+

OEM and private-label enquiries can cover the label, packaging and proposed flavor adjustments. Share the destination, required format and expected volume so the formula, artwork, documents and production scope can be agreed for your project.

How can I request samples and a quotation?+

Send the product name, destination, sales channel, intended application, selected pack and estimated quantity. We will confirm the sample arrangements, quotation, MOQ and lead time for that project. Sample and freight terms are agreed before dispatch.
