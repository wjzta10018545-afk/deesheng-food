Korean BBQ Sauce

BBQ Sauce for Marinating, Dipping & Grilling

Deesheng Food supplies Korean BBQ Sauce for marinating, dipping and grilling applications. Available formats include 200 g bottles × 24, 350 g bottles × 24, 400 g bottles × 30, and 1 kg pouches in 10- or 12-pouch cartons. Each listed option has a 12-month shelf life with ambient storage. Is this the same as Non-spicy BBQ Dipping Sauce?+

No. This page covers BBQ Sauce for marinating, dipping and grilling. The non-spicy dipping sauce is a separate product; do not assume identical formulas or flavor profiles.

Which retail and foodservice packs are available?+

Choose 200 g bottles × 24, 350 g bottles × 24, 400 g bottles × 30, or 1 kg pouches in 10- or 12-pouch cartons. Specify the carton configuration in your inquiry. Shipping data for the 12-pouch carton must not be applied to the 10-pouch carton.

What shelf life and storage apply?+

All five listed pack configurations have a 12-month shelf life with ambient storage. Request the selected product label for temperature limits and after-opening instructions.

What marinade ratio or grilling instructions should I use?+

Describe the ingredients, contact time, cooking method and portion size. Request guidance for the selected formula and evaluate a sample before fixing a production recipe; no universal application rate is specified.

What heat level should I expect?+

The packaging specification does not establish one measured heat level. Tell us your preferred flavor profile and agree on sample acceptance before ordering.

How do I check ingredients, allergens and certification?+

Request the current ingredient list, allergen declaration and any documents required for your destination. Certification must match the manufacturing site, selected product, formula and current certificate scope. A product name or pack size does not establish dietary suitability.

Can it be supplied for private label?+

OEM and private-label enquiries can cover the label, packaging and proposed flavor adjustments. Share the destination, required format and expected volume so the formula, artwork, documents and production scope can be agreed for your project.

How can I request samples and a quotation?+

Send the product name, destination, sales channel, intended application, selected pack and estimated quantity. We will confirm the sample arrangements, quotation, MOQ and lead time for that project. Sample and freight terms are agreed before dispatch.
