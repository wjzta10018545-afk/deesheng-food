Sourcing facts

Answers for buyers

These are the commercial basics most B2B buyers need before requesting a sample or quotation.

Can gochujang be private labelled?+

Yes. OEM/private-label service can cover the label, packaging and, where the project requires it, flavor adjustment.

What is the standard OEM MOQ?+

The standard MOQ is 200 cartons per item. A lower quantity may be discussed for an initial market test after the product mix is confirmed.

How is an export quotation prepared?+

Quotations are prepared after the product, pack size, quantity and destination requirements are confirmed.

Is gochujang available with HALAL documentation?+

Deesheng Food maintains SHC HALAL certification for relevant products. Qualified B2B buyers can request current documents and exact scope confirmation for the selected formula, label and order.
