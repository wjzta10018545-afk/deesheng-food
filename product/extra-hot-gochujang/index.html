Sourcing facts

Answers for buyers

These are the commercial basics most B2B buyers need before requesting a sample or quotation.

Can extra-hot gochujang be private labelled?+

Yes. OEM/private-label service can cover the label, packaging and, where the project requires it, flavor adjustment.

What is the standard OEM MOQ?+

MOQ is confirmed for the selected product, formula and pack. Ask for the quantity and SKU scope in your quotation; trial orders are assessed separately.

How is an export quotation prepared?+

Quotations are prepared after the product, pack size, quantity and destination requirements are confirmed.

Is extra-hot gochujang available with HALAL documentation?+

Please identify the product, formula, destination and required documents. We will check the actual manufacturing site, current certificate and product scope before confirming applicability to your order.

Does extra-hot gochujang have a confirmed SHU rating?+

No numerical SHU rating is listed here. Describe your target heat and request the selected formula's specification and a sample for evaluation in your recipe.

Which extra-hot gochujang packs are listed?+

The listed formats are 500 g tubs packed 20 per carton and a 14 kg foodservice carton. Confirm the extra-hot formula, final pack and label in the order specification.

How should I compare an extra-hot gochujang sample?+

Use the intended recipe and serving quantity, and compare the samples under the same preparation conditions. Approve the exact formula and pack before ordering.
