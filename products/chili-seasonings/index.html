Buyer questions

What importers usually ask

Clear commercial basics before sampling and quotation.

Can korean chili powder & seasonings be supplied under our own brand?+

Yes. Private-label and OEM service can include label artwork, export packaging and, for suitable projects, flavor or specification adjustment.

What is the normal minimum order quantity?+

MOQ depends on the selected product, formula, packaging and order plan. Confirm the quantity and SKU scope in the quotation. Trial orders are assessed separately.

Can we request samples before placing an order?+

Sample availability, costs and transport are confirmed after you share the products, packs, intended use and expected quantity. Kimchi and frozen samples require a suitable cold-chain plan; ordinary courier delivery should not be assumed.

How is an export quotation prepared?+

Product, pack size, quantity and destination are confirmed before pricing and shipment terms are agreed for the order.

Are relevant korean chili powder & seasonings covered by HALAL certification?+

Please specify the product, formula, destination and required recognition. We will check the actual manufacturing site, current certificate and product coverage before confirming whether the order meets your requirements.

Ready to shortlist?

Send your channel, pack and estimated quantity.

We will recommend a practical starting mix and confirm sample or quotation details for your market.
