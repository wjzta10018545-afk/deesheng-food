Buyer selection guide

Choose Korean sauces for wholesale and private label

Compare the product, pack and order quantity for your channel. Some standard-pack sauce OEM projects use 200 cartons per item as a quotation starting point. Confirm the applicable SKU, formula and pack; trials are assessed separately.

01

Pick an application

Compare fried-chicken, fire-noodle, bibimbap, tteokbokki, BBQ and soup sauces by menu use and target heat level. Classic, creamy and non-spicy variants have separate specifications.

02

Match the pack to the channel

Review retail bottles and 1 kg sauce pouches. Gochujang also has 500 g tubs and 14 kg foodservice cartons. Sizes and carton quantities are product-specific.

03

Confirm project scope

Ask about samples, flavor adjustment and private label. HALAL documents and coverage must be checked against the selected formula and order.

Buyer questions

What importers usually ask

Clear commercial basics before sampling and quotation.

Can korean sauces be supplied under our own brand?+

Yes. Private-label and OEM service can include label artwork, export packaging and, for suitable projects, flavor or specification adjustment.

What is the normal minimum order quantity?+

MOQ depends on the selected product, formula, packaging and order plan. Confirm the quantity and SKU scope in the quotation. Trial orders are assessed separately.

Can we request samples before placing an order?+

Sample availability, costs and transport are confirmed after you share the products, packs, intended use and expected quantity. Kimchi and frozen samples require a suitable cold-chain plan; ordinary courier delivery should not be assumed.

How is an export quotation prepared?+

Product, pack size, quantity and destination are confirmed before pricing and shipment terms are agreed for the order.

Are relevant korean sauces covered by HALAL certification?+

Please specify the product, formula, destination and required recognition. We will check the actual manufacturing site, current certificate and product coverage before confirming whether the order meets your requirements.

Which Korean sauces are suitable for restaurants or private label?+

The range includes fried-chicken glazes, buldak, bibimbap, tteokbokki and Korean BBQ sauces. Share the application, flavor target, pack size, market and estimated volume to shortlist foodservice or retail options.

Do all sauce flavors and pack sizes have the same shelf life?+

No. Use the exact product and pack specification. Honey mustard, for example, has both 9- and 12-month configurations. Where a catalogue field asks for SKU confirmation, request the current specification rather than assuming a standard shelf life or storage condition.

Are fire noodle sauces finished noodles or branded instant-noodle products?+

The classic, creamy and mild fire noodle detail pages describe Deesheng sauces for noodle applications. They are sauce products, not finished instant noodles. Confirm the exact recipe, ingredients, heat and packaging for your own brand or foodservice project.
