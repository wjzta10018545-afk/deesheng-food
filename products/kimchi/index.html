Buyer questions

What importers usually ask

Clear commercial basics before sampling and quotation.

Can korean kimchi be supplied under our own brand?+

Yes. Private-label and OEM service can include label artwork, export packaging and, for suitable projects, flavor or specification adjustment.

What is the normal minimum order quantity?+

The standard OEM MOQ is 200 cartons per item. A lower quantity may be discussed for an initial trial after the selected products and packing are confirmed.

Can we request samples before placing an order?+

Yes. Qualified B2B buyers can request samples after sharing their company, sales channel, target products and expected order quantity.

How is an export quotation prepared?+

Product, pack size, quantity and destination are confirmed before pricing and shipment terms are agreed for the order.

Are relevant korean kimchi covered by HALAL certification?+

Deesheng Food maintains SHC HALAL certification for relevant products. Qualified B2B buyers can request current documents and exact scope confirmation for the selected product, formula and order.

Ready to shortlist?

Send your channel, pack and estimated quantity.

We will recommend a practical starting mix and confirm sample or quotation details for your market.
