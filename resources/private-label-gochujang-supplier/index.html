How to Source Private-Label Gochujang

What should I check when choosing a private-label gochujang supplier?

A buyer-focused checklist covering product profile, 500 g and 14 kg packs, private label, MOQ, samples and export quotation. The sections below explain the practical sourcing and commercial decisions in detail.

01

Define how the gochujang will be used

Retail gochujang must work as a consumer-facing cooking paste with clear instructions and market-appropriate label content. Foodservice gochujang is judged more heavily on consistency, cost in use, application performance and bulk handling.

State whether the target is bibimbap, marinades, stir-fry, soup bases, dipping sauces or further sauce manufacturing.

02

Select the heat and flavor profile

Gochujang is not defined by heat alone. Buyers should evaluate fermented aroma, sweetness, salt, chili character, color, smoothness and aftertaste. Deesheng Food offers a standard Korean-style gochujang and an extra-hot option for spice-led markets.

03

Match the pack to the channel

A 500 g tub packed 20 units per carton is a practical format for retail and smaller foodservice customers. A 14 kg foodservice carton supports restaurant chains, central kitchens and further processing. Final net weight, carton data and label specification should be confirmed before quotation.

04

Check supplier qualification against the project

Deesheng Food’s export program is supported by BRCGS Grade A, HACCP, HALAL and OU Kosher certification. Buyers should request documents relevant to the selected item and destination, then review the product specification, ingredient list, allergens, shelf life, storage and label.

05

Sampling and commercial terms

Qualified B2B buyers should share company details, sales channel, selected pack and estimated quantity before requesting samples. The standard OEM MOQ is 200 cartons per item, with first-trial flexibility discussed case by case. Export quotations are prepared after the project details and shipment requirements are confirmed.

Q

Buyer FAQ

What private-label gochujang packs are available?+

Common options are 500 g tubs packed 20 per carton and 14 kg foodservice cartons.

How long is the shelf life?+

The standard ambient shelf life shown for Deesheng gochujang is 12 months under recommended storage conditions.

Is extra-hot gochujang available?+

Yes. A hotter gochujang option is available for buyers targeting a stronger heat profile.
