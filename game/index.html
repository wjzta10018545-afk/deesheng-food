How to play

  1. Tap the Raw Chicken box to drop chicken into a fryer.
  2. When it turns golden ✨, tap the fryer. Too long and it burns!
  3. Tap the sauce shown in the customer’s bubble.
  4. Add the toppings and kimchi side if they asked for it.
  5. Tap the customer to serve. Faster = bigger tips. Wrong order = angry customer.
